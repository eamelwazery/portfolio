Pack Process Simulation

Pre-hire Candidates 30 min + 15 min assessment English
Challenge: Candidates needed a realistic preview of the pack role before joining, so they arrive on day one already understanding how the work is done.
Solution: A fully interactive Storyline simulation with a hands-on "Scan and Pack" station — learners review the order, drag each item to the scanner, and place it in the recommended box, exactly like the real workflow.
Impact: Candidates build an accurate mental model of the job before hire — smoother, faster, more confident ramp-up.

Ship Process Simulation

Pre-hire Candidates 30 min + 15 min assessment English
Challenge: Candidates needed to understand the full Ship Dock flow — and the real decisions a sorter makes — before joining, so they arrive ready for the role.
Solution: An interactive Storyline simulation that walks learners through the order's journey, sorting & palletizing, and a hands-on decision-making diverting task — "Leave It" or "Take It" based on assigned zones — mirroring the real judgment calls on the floor.
Impact: Candidates don't just watch — they practice the decisions, building real readiness and confidence before day one.

Bin Etiquette Simulation

Stowers Stow Compliance English
Challenge: Stowers needed to master correct bin etiquette — where they can and can't stow, how the system's rules work, and how to keep inventory organized and safe.
Solution: A hands-on drag-and-drop simulation where learners follow the Stow Map, respect ASIN capacity limits (max 4 ASINs, locked bins), and get instant feedback — mirroring the real system filters that block incorrect stows.
Impact: Stowers internalize the rules by doing, reducing stow errors and keeping bins organized, safe, and compliant.

Training Readiness

Learning Team 15 min English

TDR Process (Trailer-Dock-Release)

Dock Associates 30 min English
Challenge: Dock associates perform the Trailer-Dock-Release process on trucks — a safety-critical task where every step must be done correctly and in the right order.
Solution: A self-paced Storyline simulation with full voiceover and hands-on practice at every step — learners explore dock types (Swing, Telescopic, Open), place safety cones correctly, and operate the dock control panel, step by step.

TDR Assessment

English & Arabic 15 min Pass score 80%
